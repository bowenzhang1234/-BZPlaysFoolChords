Name a file after a syllable, a chord, or a card id.

wah.wav, cho.ogg, and ki.wav match the three melody voices.
C.wav, G.ogg, and Am.wav match those chords.
WAH_CHO_KI.wav matches that card.

Set instrument = samples in chords.cfg and restart.
The first file that exists is used, in the order syllable, chord, card id.
Wav and ogg are both accepted. A recording of your own voice is fine.
